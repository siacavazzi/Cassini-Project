# Taste call 3, round 4: smooth terminator (Sam, 11-17: the light-to-dark transition read as a gray band)

Each sheet: Today | L revised (sent 11-16) | New, with a 1:1 crop at the terminator (yellow box) below.

Cause: the local contrast compares each pixel with the average of its neighbourhood, and that average was taken over the lit pixels only. Along a terminator it therefore sat on the bright side, so the whole fall from light to dark was pulled darker (on the 2007 full disk up to 24 levels darker, with a lighter edge just inside it): the gray band.
Fix, as a general rule: the average now covers everything but the sky, and the change eases off on a steep smooth fall (as the moons' fix in PR 530 does). Brightness changes along the terminator are now within about ±5 levels of today, and the belts keep about 90% of L revised's added detail.
