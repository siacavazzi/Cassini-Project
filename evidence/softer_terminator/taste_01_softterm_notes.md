# Taste call 1 follow-up: softer terminator, Titan haze left alone (strength 1.6)

Strength 1.6 (Sam's pick). Two changes against PR #495 as first shown: the disk detail filter eases off across the terminator, where a moon's brightness falls off steeply from light to dark (Sam: the transition looked overprocessed), and Titan's haze skips the disk filter (Sam: nothing to sharpen there), except frames in Titan's CB3 surface window, where the surface does show detail; Titan keeps the gentle noise-aware fine sharpening. The rest of each body keeps its detail. Moons in front of Saturn's disk keep the fine sharpening only, as before. Pictures: main's own pipeline, finished with the branch's steps.
